What the result means

If the device replies to BEETLE's read-only 0x50 HELLO with 0x60, the BEETLE application is running. If the Hub simultaneously calls it another firmware's update-loader, that points to Hub-side classification or port identification, not proof that the loader is actually running. If the application handshake is absent, we cannot tell from identity alone whether another loader is active or USB traffic was interrupted.
